About Chris

Compassionate counselling grounded in evidence.

In-person counselling in Brandon, Manitoba.

I believe everyone deserves to be heard, understood, and treated with compassion.

As a counsellor and professor, I bring both practical experience and a strong academic foundation to my work. I am committed to using evidence-based approaches to help adolescents and adults better understand themselves, navigate difficult experiences, and work toward meaningful change.

I am particularly comfortable working with individuals experiencing depression, anxiety, and trauma. My goal is to create a safe, respectful, and collaborative space where clients can explore their concerns and develop strategies that support their well-being.

Counselling is not about telling people how to live their lives. It is about working alongside them, helping them develop greater understanding, and supporting them as they move toward the life they want.

My Counselling Approach

Evidence-based, compassionate, and collaborative

I use current, evidence-based cognitive behavioural approaches, tailoring counselling to each client’s needs, goals, strengths, and circumstances.

I place particular emphasis on Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT), which supports psychological flexibility, values-based living, self-understanding, and meaningful action. I also draw from a range of complementary evidence-informed approaches, depending on the concerns and goals of each client.

I am currently developing an integrative counselling approach that brings together several complementary theoretical foundations and emphasizes compassionate, collaborative, trauma-informed, and practical support. This developing model is intended to help clients better understand themselves, navigate challenges, and move toward lives that are meaningful to them.
